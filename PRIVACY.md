# NumbCrunch Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 16, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the NumbCrunch game for Android (package
`io.keval.apps.numbcrunch`), published on Google Play by Keval Langalia ("we",
"us"), handles information. It applies to the app only.

## Summary

NumbCrunch does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal
information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app
purchases, and no internet access. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

## Information the App Collects

**None.** NumbCrunch does not request the Android internet permission, so it
cannot send any information from your device to us or to anyone else. It does
not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, device
identifiers, or advertising ID.

## Information Stored Only on Your Device

To work as a game, NumbCrunch keeps a small amount of information on your
device. We cannot see or access any of it.

- **Settings:** whether background music and sound effects are turned on.
- **Tutorial progress:** whether you have completed the first-launch tutorial.
- **The current round:** the hidden number, your guesses, and the game mode are
  kept while a round is in progress, so Android can restore the round if it
  closes the app in the background. In Double Player mode, the number your
  friend hides is held only in the app's memory and is lost when the app closes.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the app's storage in your
device's settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Device Backups

If you have turned on backup for your Android device, Android may include the
app's settings and tutorial progress in your device backup, which Google stores
in your Google account. This backup is managed by Android and Google under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we cannot
access it. You can control backups in your device's settings.

## Google Play

NumbCrunch is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information
when you download, install, or use apps from Google Play, as described in
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). As the
developer, we receive only aggregated statistics from Google Play Console, such
as install counts, and anonymized crash and performance reports from users who
have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These reports do
not identify you.

## External Links

The About screen contains links to the game's public source code on GitHub and
to its open-source license. Opening a link takes you out of the app to a
website that has its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

## Children's Privacy

NumbCrunch is a family game that can be enjoyed by children. Because the app
collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal
information from children of any age.

## Changes to This Policy

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this policy and
its effective date before the change is released. Previous versions are
available in the
[history of this file](https://github.com/theKeval/NumbCrunch/commits/main/PRIVACY.md).
